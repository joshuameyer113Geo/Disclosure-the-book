# Disclosure: Modern and Military UFO Cases (Research Brief)

This is a research brief for *Disclosure*, a realistic, sociological history of ufology. **Reconciled 2026-10-28** against Eden Historian's fact sheet (`history/MODERN_CASES_FACTS.md`) and timeline (`history/TIMELINE.md`), both built from live web checks. Where the original draft and the historian disagreed, the historian's sourced fact is used and the disagreement is noted inline as **[Draft said: …]**. Draft details the historian did not cover are kept but marked **[unverified]**.

**Historian tags:** **Reported fact** = the event or record is documented (not that the object was extraterrestrial). **In between** = a real report whose nature is contested. **Lore** = uncorroborated, hoaxed, or interpretive; treated here as culture and reception, not evidence.
**Draft labels kept:** **[C]** = documents or several independent sources. **[U]** = uncorroborated or witness-only. **Profit** = whether the witness made money from the story.

## 1. Kenneth Arnold — June 24, 1947, near Mount Rainier, Washington
- **Historian tag:** In between. *Sighting and coverage documented; objects never identified.*
- **Before:** Private pilot with about 4,000 flight hours, member of an Idaho search-and-rescue unit (Smithsonian). [C] Draft detail that he sold fire-control equipment: [unverified].

- **Aftermath:** He wrote a confidential report for the Army Air Forces (July 1947) and later spoke of "embarrassment... by misquotes." [C] An August 1947 Gallup poll found 90% of Americans had heard of flying saucers. [C] Co-wrote *The Coming of the Saucers* (1952) with Ray Palmer: [unverified].

## Patterns for the sociological thread
1. Most early witnesses (Arnold, Zamora, the D.C. controllers, Mantell) made no money; Arnold spoke of embarrassment from misquotes.
2. Government responses moved from a 1947 announcement and retraction, to the Robertson Panel's 1953 advice to reduce public "mystery," to formal study (AARO, from July 2022).
3. Claims of suppression grow decades after events (Marcel 1978; Salas going public 1996).
4. Since 2017, the central witnesses are military professionals with sensor data. Profit stays small except for Lazar, Walton, and Elizondo.
5. The lore cases (Hills, Walton, Lazar) are best read as reception history: books, TV films, Hollywood, and internet events.
