# Disclosure: Modern and Military UFO Cases (Research Brief)

This is a research brief for *Disclosure*, a realistic, sociological history of ufology. The facts here come from what was reported at the time and from public records. Aftermath claims are given as **claims** and credited to the person who made them. Labels: **[C]** = backed by documents or several independent sources. **[U]** = uncorroborated, or the only source is the witness. **Profit** = whether the witness made money from their story.

Sources: I list the best primary or near-primary URLs I know. The Eden Historian should check every link before publication (see the list at the end).

## 1. Kenneth Arnold — June 24, 1947
- **Before:** Private pilot and businessman from Boise, Idaho, who sold fire-control equipment. He was an experienced mountain flier and a search-and-rescue volunteer. [C]
- **Sighting:** He was flying his CallAir A-2 near Mount Rainier, Washington, and joined the search for a missing C-46 transport. He reported nine shiny objects moving fast between Rainier and Mount Adams. He said they moved "like a saucer if you skip it across water," and the press turned that into "flying saucer." [C]
- **Explanations offered:** Mirage, pelicans, meteor fragments, and spotting distant aircraft have all been suggested. None has been settled. Project Blue Book lists the case as unidentified/mirage.
- **Aftermath:** He became a public figure overnight. He later said the ridicule was hard on him and that he regretted reporting. [C, his own statements] He co-wrote *The Coming of the Saucers* (1952) with Ray Palmer.
- **Profit:** Modest: a book and paid talks.
- Sources: https://www.project1947.com/fig/1947c.htm ; Blue Book file at https://www.archives.gov/research/military/air-force/ufos ; https://www.fold3.com/title/480/project-blue-book-ufo-investigations

## 2. Roswell — Maj. Jesse Marcel, July 1947
- **Before:** Jesse A. Marcel was the intelligence officer of the 509th Bomb Group at Roswell Army Air Field, New Mexico. [C]
- **Event:** Rancher W.W. "Mac" Brazel found debris on the Foster ranch. On July 8, 1947, the RAAF press release said it had recovered a "flying disc." Within a day, Gen. Roger Ramey's office in Fort Worth said it was a weather balloon. Marcel was photographed with foil and balsa debris. [C]
- **Explanations offered:** In 1994–95 the USAF report said the debris came from Project Mogul, a classified balloon array for detecting Soviet nuclear tests. The 1997 report says later "alien body" accounts came from crash-test dummies and from incidents misplaced in time. GAO (1995) found that some RAAF records from that period had been destroyed. [C]
- **Aftermath:** Marcel retired as a Lt. Col. He went public in 1978 (with Stanton Friedman) and said the material was "not of this earth" and that the balloon story was a cover-up. [U, his claim] Researchers later found exaggerations in his service record claims (his pilot status and degree). [C, documented by critics] His son, Jesse Marcel Jr., backed his account.
- **Profit:** Marcel Sr. made little. Roswell later became a tourist industry.
- Sources: USAF *The Roswell Report* (1995) https://media.defense.gov/2010/Dec/01/2001330156/-1/-1/0/AFD-101201-038.pdf ; GAO/NSIAD-95-187 https://www.gao.gov/products/nsiad-95-187 ; *Roswell Daily Record*, July 8, 1947

## 3. Capt. Thomas Mantell — January 7, 1948
- **Before:** Kentucky Air National Guard pilot, age 25, and a WWII C-47 pilot who flew at Normandy. [C]
- **Sighting:** Godman Field (Fort Knox) control tower reported a large object in the sky. Mantell, flying a P-51, climbed after it to about 25,000 ft without oxygen equipment. He blacked out and crashed near Franklin, Kentucky, and died. [C]
- **Explanations offered:** Blue Book first said Venus. It later concluded the object was most likely a classified Navy Skyhook balloon. [C]
- **Aftermath:** He was the first death linked to a UFO case. Rumors of a "hostile saucer" followed. Those rumors are not supported. [U]
- **Profit:** None (killed).
- Sources: Blue Book case file (Fold3/NARA, as above) ; https://www.nationalguard.mil (Ky ANG history) ; Ruppelt, *The Report on Unidentified Flying Objects* (1956), https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/17346

## 4. Washington, D.C. — July 19–20 and 26–27, 1952
- **Who:** Air traffic controllers at Washington National Airport, including senior controller Harry Barnes, plus Andrews AFB radar operators and airline pilots. [C]
- **Event:** Unknown radar returns appeared over the capital on two weekends. Jets were scrambled, and some pilots reported lights that disappeared when approached. [C]
- **Explanations offered:** On July 29, 1952, Maj. Gen. John Samford held the largest Pentagon press conference since WWII and blamed temperature inversions. The CAA's 1953 study agreed. Some controllers disputed this. [C]
- **Aftermath:** It helped lead to the CIA's Robertson Panel (January 1953), which recommended "debunking" to reduce public interest. [C, declassified]
- **Profit:** None.
- Sources: https://www.cia.gov/readingroom/document/cia-rdp81r00560r000100030027-0 (Robertson Panel materials) ; https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/cias-role-in-the-study-of-ufos-1947-90/ ; *Washington Post*, July 28, 1952

## 5. Betty & Barney Hill — September 19–20, 1961
- **Before:** Portsmouth, New Hampshire. Betty was a social worker. Barney was a postal worker and an NAACP civil-rights activist. They were an interracial couple, which was rare at the time. [C]
- **Sighting:** Driving home from Canada on U.S. Route 3 in the White Mountains, they reported a light that followed them, a craft with figures seen through binoculars, and "missing time." Under hypnosis with Dr. Benjamin Simon (1964) they described being abducted. They reported to Pease AFB, and Blue Book logged the report. [C that they reported; the abduction is U]
- **Explanations offered:** Simon thought it was most likely a shared fantasy that came from Betty's dreams. Others pointed to the planet Jupiter or to an aircraft beacon.
- **Aftermath:** John Fuller's *The Interrupted Journey* (1966) and a 1975 TV movie followed. Barney died in 1969 at age 46 of a cerebral hemorrhage. There is no credible claim tying his death to the case. They faced publicity and some ridicule.
- **Profit:** Modest (they shared in the book).
- Sources: Betty & Barney Hill papers, Univ. of New Hampshire https://www.library.unh.edu/find/archives/collections/betty-and-barney-hill-papers-1961-2006 ; Blue Book (NARA)

## 6. Lonnie Zamora — Socorro, NM, April 24, 1964
- **Before:** Socorro police officer and a well-regarded local. [C]
- **Sighting:** While chasing a speeder, he heard a roar and saw an egg-shaped craft on legs with two small figures beside it. It lifted off with a flame. Investigators found burn marks and imprint marks. [C that the traces were reported]
- **Explanations offered:** Blue Book's Hector Quintanilla left it "unidentified," one of its best-known unsolved cases. J. Allen Hynek investigated it. Theories include a hoax by New Mexico Tech students (a letter from Linus Pauling's associate is cited) or a lunar-module test. [U]
- **Aftermath:** Zamora was overwhelmed by attention, avoided publicity, and later worked at a gas station. He reportedly found the experience distressing. [C, press]
- **Profit:** None.
- Sources: Blue Book Socorro file https://www.archives.gov/research/military/air-force/ufos ; FBI Vault https://vault.fbi.gov/UFO

## 7. Malmstrom AFB — March 1967 (Robert Salas)
- **Before:** Lt. Robert Salas was a Minuteman missile launch officer at Malmstrom AFB, Montana. [C]
- **Event:** Salas says that on March 24, 1967, guards reported a red glowing object over Oscar Flight and that missiles went offline. Documents confirm that 10 missiles at Echo Flight shut down on March 16, 1967. The Air Force attributed that to an electrical fault. [C for the Echo shutdown; U for the UFO and for any Oscar Flight shutdown]
- **Aftermath:** He says he was told to sign a non-disclosure order. [U] He testified at the 2010 National Press Club event (Robert Hastings) and to the 2023 public "UFO hearings." He co-wrote *Faded Giant* and *Unidentified* (2014).
- **Profit:** Small (books and talks).
- Sources: Echo Flight unit history (declassified) via https://www.theblackvault.com ; https://www.cufos.org ; National Press Club, September 27, 2010 (C-SPAN https://www.c-span.org/video/?295592-1/ufos-nuclear-weapons)

## 8. Travis Walton — November 5, 1975
- **Before:** 22-year-old logging worker on a forest-thinning crew under Mike Rogers, Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest, Arizona. [C]
- **Sighting:** Six crewmates said Walton walked toward a glowing craft, was hit by a beam, and that they drove off. He was missing for five days, and a police search followed. [C that he was missing and the crew reported this]
- **Explanations offered:** Some propose a hoax so the crew could escape a behind-schedule Forest Service contract (Philip Klass). Polygraph results were mixed (the first one failed, later ones passed).
- **Aftermath:** He faced ridicule locally and nationally. The book *The Walton Experience* (1978) was adapted into the film *Fire in the Sky* (1993). Crewmates still stand by the account. [C, interviews]
- **Profit:** Yes, moderate (book, film rights, a festival, talks).
- Sources: Navajo County Sheriff reports via https://www.travis-walton.com ; *Arizona Republic*, November 1975 ; Klass, *UFOs: The Public Deceived*

## 9. Rendlesham Forest — December 26–28, 1980
- **Before:** RAF Bentwaters/Woodbridge, Suffolk, UK, a USAF base. Lt. Col. Charles Halt was deputy base commander. SSgt Jim Penniston and A1C John Burroughs were security police. [C]
- **Event:** Penniston and Burroughs went into the forest to look into lights and reported a craft. Penniston later claimed he touched it. Two nights later, Halt led a patrol, recorded it on audio tape, and wrote a memo to the UK MoD (January 13, 1981) describing lights, radiation readings, and imprint marks. [C: Halt memo and tape]
- **Explanations offered:** Orfordness lighthouse, stars, and police lights (Ian Ridpath). The MoD found "no defence significance." [C]
- **Aftermath:** Halt retired as a colonel. In 2010 he signed an affidavit saying it was extraterrestrial and that there had been a cover-up. [U] Penniston said in 2010 that he had written down "binary code" in 1980. [U, it appeared decades later] Burroughs won a VA disability claim in 2015 with help from Sen. John McCain's office. He says his injuries came from the incident. [C that the claim was granted; the cause is U]
- **Profit:** Modest (books and talks).
- Sources: Halt memo, UK National Archives DEFE 24/1948 https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk ; https://www.ianridpath.com/ufo/rendlesham.html

## 10. Japan Air Lines Flight 1628 — November 17, 1986
- **Before:** Capt. Kenju Terauchi was a veteran JAL pilot and former JASDF pilot. He was flying a Boeing 747 cargo flight from Paris to Tokyo via Anchorage. [C]
- **Sighting:** Over Alaska, the crew reported huge lights and a "walnut-shaped" mothership. FAA Anchorage logged radar contacts, which were disputed. [C]
- **Explanations offered:** FAA analyst Richard Callahan later said the CIA took the data. [U] The FAA concluded radar returns were split images and Jupiter/Mars, possibly with lights from the planet's distance. [C]
- **Aftermath:** JAL reportedly took Terauchi off flying duty and gave him a desk job. He was later reinstated. [C, press reports]
- **Profit:** None known.
- Sources: FAA documents https://www.faa.gov ; FOIA via https://www.theblackvault.com ; *New York Times*, January 1987

## 11. Phoenix Lights — March 13, 1997
- **Who:** Thousands of witnesses, including Arizona Gov. Fife Symington III. [C]
- **Event:** A V-shaped set of lights moved across the state in the evening. Later (around 10 pm), a separate set of hovering lights appeared. [C]
- **Explanations offered:** The USAF/Maryland ANG said the later lights were illumination flares dropped at the Barry Goldwater Range. The V shape is unexplained or disputed (some suggest aircraft in formation).
- **Aftermath:** Symington mocked the event at a press conference with an aide dressed as an alien. In 2007 he said he had seen a craft himself and believed it was "otherworldly." [U] He was convicted in 1997 of bank fraud (overturned in 1999, then pardoned by Clinton in 2001). That is unrelated but matters for context.
- **Profit:** None from the sighting.
- Sources: *Arizona Republic* coverage ; Symington, CNN op-ed November 9, 2007 https://www.cnn.com/2007/TECH/space/11/09/simington.ufocommentary/

## 12. Bob Lazar — 1989
- **Before:** Claims to have physics degrees from MIT and Caltech. Neither school has a record of him. [C that no records exist] He worked as a technician at Los Alamos, which a 1982 *Los Alamos Monitor* article confirms. [C]
- **Claim:** In KLAS-TV interviews (George Knapp, 1989), he said he reverse-engineered alien craft at "S-4" near Area 51 for EG&G/the Navy. [U]
- **Aftermath:** He says his records were erased and that he was threatened. [U] He was convicted on a pandering charge (1990) and later in an FBI raid tied to chemical sales (2006 plea). His W-2 from the Navy (Dept. of Naval Intelligence) is cited by supporters. Its meaning is disputed.
- **Profit:** Yes: the documentary *Bob Lazar: Area 51 & Flying Saucers* (2018) and media. He runs United Nuclear, a separate business.
- Sources: KLAS-TV archive (Knapp) ; *Los Alamos Monitor*, June 27, 1982 ; https://www.skeptic.com

## 13. USS Nimitz "Tic Tac" — November 14, 2004
- **Before:** Cdr. David Fravor was commanding officer of VFA-41 and a Top Gun-trained pilot. Lt. Cdr. Alex Dietrich was a VFA-41 pilot. Senior Chief Kevin Day was an operations specialist on the USS Princeton's SPY-1 radar. [C]
- **Sighting:** The Princeton tracked anomalous objects for days off Southern California. Fravor and Dietrich saw a white Tic Tac-shaped object about 40 ft long, with no wings, above a churning patch of water. It matched Fravor's maneuvers and sped away. Chad Underwood's FLIR1 video was later recorded. [C: Navy confirmed the video is authentic]
- **Explanations offered:** Unknown. AARO still lists the case as unresolved. Skeptics (e.g., Mick West) suggest sensor artifacts.
- **Aftermath:** It became public in the *NYT* (December 16, 2017). Fravor testified to the House Oversight subcommittee on July 26, 2023. Dietrich has spoken, including on *60 Minutes* (2021). Day reported personal distress and left the Navy.
- **Profit:** Little. Fravor has done podcasts.
- Sources: https://www.nytimes.com/2017/12/16/us/politics/pentagon-program-ufo-harry-reid.html ; House hearing https://oversight.house.gov/hearing/unidentified-anomalous-phenomena-implications-on-national-security-public-safety-and-government-transparency/ ; Navy video release https://www.navair.navy.mil/foia/documents

## 14. USS Theodore Roosevelt — 2014–15 (Ryan Graves)
- **Before:** Lt. Ryan Graves was an F/A-18F pilot with VFA-11 out of Virginia Beach. [C]
- **Sightings:** Pilots reported near-daily objects over the Atlantic training ranges once radars were upgraded. One near-collision involved a "cube inside a sphere." The GIMBAL and GOFAST videos came from this period. [C: Navy confirmed]
- **Explanations offered:** Unknown. Some may be balloons or drones (AARO's 2023 analysis attributed some reports to balloons; GOFAST's speed was assessed as low via parallax).
- **Aftermath:** Graves testified on July 26, 2023, and founded Americans for Safe Aerospace (a nonprofit). He reports stigma against pilots who report. [C, his testimony]
- **Profit:** Indirect only.
- Sources: Graves testimony via the House Oversight link above ; *NYT*, May 26, 2019 https://www.nytimes.com/2019/05/26/us/politics/ufo-sightings-navy-pilots.html

## 15. David Grusch — July 26, 2023
- **Before:** Former NGA and NRO intelligence officer and USAF veteran (Afghanistan), who served on the UAP Task Force. [C]
- **Claims:** Under oath, he said the U.S. runs a decades-long crash retrieval and reverse-engineering program, holds "non-human biologics," and that people were hurt protecting it. These are secondhand, from interviews with others. [U] He filed an IG whistleblower complaint (2022), which the ICIG found "credible and urgent." That finding covers the complaint process, not the claims themselves. [C]
- **Aftermath:** He reported retaliation. [U] Media reported a 2018 police mental-health hold, which he has spoken about. AARO's Historical Record Report Vol. 1 (March 2024) found no evidence of reverse-engineering programs. [C]
- **Profit:** None evident.
- Sources: https://www.thedebrief.org/intelligence-officials-say-u-s-has-retrieved-non-human-craft/ ; House testimony link above ; AARO report https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/AARO_Historical_Record_Report_Volume_1_2024.pdf

## 16. Luis "Lue" Elizondo
- **Before:** Career Army counterintelligence officer and DoD official. [C]
- **Claim:** He says he led AATIP (2008–2017) and resigned in protest in October 2017. The Pentagon has given conflicting statements on his role. [Disputed] He joined To The Stars Academy. The *NYT* 2017 story used him as a source.
- **Aftermath:** He filed an IG complaint (2021) alleging a smear campaign. [U] His book *Imminent* (2024) became a bestseller, with claims of threats and of a "Legacy program." [U]
- **Profit:** Yes (book, TV with History Channel's *Unidentified*).
- Sources: NYT 2017 link above ; https://www.aaro.mil ; Pentagon statements via *The Intercept* (2019)

## Patterns for the sociological thread
1. Most early witnesses (Arnold, Zamora, the D.C. controllers, Mantell) made no money and were ridiculed.
2. Government responses moved from openness (1947), to managing debunking (Robertson Panel, 1953), to formal study (AARO, from 2022).
3. Claims of suppression grow decades after the events (Marcel in 1978, Halt in 2010, Penniston's 2010 binary code).
4. Since 2017, the witnesses are military professionals backed by sensor data. Profit is still small, except for Lazar, Walton, and Elizondo.
