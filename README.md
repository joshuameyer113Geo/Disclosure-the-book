# Disclosure

A realistic historical documentary book on the UFO phenomenon: a sociological read on ufology, from ancient lore to modern military cases and the government disclosure debate.

## How the book is organized
- `manuscript/`: part and chapter folders (placeholders; not drafted yet)
- `outline/OUTLINE.md`: the planned structure
- `research/`: ANCIENT_TIMELINE.md, MODERN_CASES.md (coming), sources/
- `rules/STORY_RULES.md`: the canon rules
- `notes/REUSE_REVIEW.md`: reuse review notes
- `imagery/`: timeline images (still to come)

- **Part II: Modern and Military Cases**, TBD pending MODERN_CASES.md
- **Part III: The Cultural and Sociological Read** (plan)
- **Part IV: Government Disclosure** (plan)

## Research-first method
Research comes before writing. Story drafting waits for Eden Historian's dated timeline with imagery attached.

## Canon rules
Real people keep real names. Real reported events and established UFO lore are canon. Nothing is invented: no fake names or places. Artistic symbolism and creative filling-in are allowed only when grounded in researched context, with sources cited.
