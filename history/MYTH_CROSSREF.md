# MYTH_CROSSREF: Myths and their modern UFO and ancient-astronaut readings

Claim IDs MX-001 to MX-021. Tags: **Fact**, **Contested**, **Lore**. Each lore claim names who made it and when. Every entry gives the primary source, when the UFO reading arose, who made it, and what scholars say. All URLs were re-checked live on Oct 28, 2026 (regenerated after the workspace wipe). ## 1. Norse

### [MX-001] Primary sources: the Poetic Edda and Snorri's Prose Edda (1200s Iceland). **Fact**
- Both are 13th-century Icelandic texts written in a Christian setting. In his Prologue, Snorri euhemerizes the Æsir: they were human kings from Asia (Troy/Phrygia) who were later worshipped as gods.
- Sources: https://www.jasoncolavito.com/blog/review-of-ancient-aliens-s05e11-the-viking-gods (quotes Snorri's Prologue) ; https://www.jasoncolavito.com/blog/review-of-ancient-aliens-s21e16-mysteries-of-the-norse-gods ; https://www.norsemyth.org/2013/04/notes-for-viking-gods-part-one.html

### [MX-002] Thor, Odin, Asgard as aliens: *Ancient Aliens* S05E11 "The Viking Gods" (History, aired Apr 2013). **Lore, made by Giorgio Tsoukalos, David Childress, Philip Coppens, Jason Martell and William Henry, 2013**
- Claims made on the show:
  - Mjölnir is a kinetic weapon (Coppens).
  - Bifröst may be a wormhole to another planet.
  - Odin's ravens are drones (Childress).
  - Odin's throne is a spaceship "captain's chair" (Martell).
  - Thor's belt is an exoskeleton (Coppens, Martell).
  - Valhalla is a space station (Tsoukalos).
  - Dwarves are the Greys (Childress, Coppens).
- Later re-run: S21E16 "Mysteries of the Norse Gods." William Henry calls Odin's throne alien technology and Gungnir a guided missile; Tsoukalos calls the myths "misunderstood technology."
- What scholars and critics say: Colavito notes that Snorri's euhemerism contradicts any literal reading, and that the gods share Indo-European roots (Týr is cognate with Zeus and Dyaus). In the Eddas the nine worlds are planes joined by a world tree, not planets, and Bifröst is a built structure.
- Sources: https://www.springfieldspringfield.co.uk/view_episode_scripts.php?episode=s05e11&tv-show=ancient-aliens ; https://www.jasoncolavito.com/blog/review-of-ancient-aliens-s05e11-the-viking-gods ; https://www.jasoncolavito.com/blog/review-of-ancient-aliens-s21e16-mysteries-of-the-norse-gods

### [MX-003] Von Däniken or Sitchin on Asgard specifically. **GAP**
- No sourced passage was found in which von Däniken or Sitchin treats Asgard. Martell's on-air ideas derive from Sitchin by his own account (per Colavito), but that is not a Sitchin claim about Norse myth. Do not attribute one without a source.

### [MX-004] Nordic tall-blond aliens ↔ Norse gods. **Contested / GAP**
- The "Nordic" alien type comes from the 1950s contactees (AO-019, T-041). The label itself is dated only to the 1980s (AO-020). No source found here draws an explicit Norse-god = Nordic-alien parallel and names its originator. The trope's documented racial and ideological echoes are covered in AO-005, AO-014 and AO-022.

## 2. Greek

### [MX-005] Talos: bronze guardian of Crete (Apollonius, *Argonautica* 4.1635–88; 5th-c. BC vase paintings). **Fact (the sources)**
- Talos was made by Hephaestus, had a single vein of ichor sealed by a bolt at the ankle, and was destroyed with Medea's help.
- Sources: https://doi.org/10.4000/ephaistos.10885 ; https://assets.press.princeton.edu/chapters/i14162.pdf

### [MX-006] Talos as a robot from ancient astronauts. **Lore, made by Erich von Däniken, *Odyssey of the Gods* (c.1999 per Colavito), and David Childress, *Technology of the Gods* (2000)**
- What scholars say:
  - A. B. Cook (*Zeus*, vol. 1, 1914) explained Talos as Cretan Zeus Tallaios and read the vein-and-bolt detail as lost-wax bronze casting.
  - Adrienne Mayor (*Gods and Robots*, Princeton 2018) reads Talos as ancient Greeks *imagining* artificial life ("biotechne"), not as contact.
  - Colavito argues Talos became robot-like only through later rationalization. That is a scholarly dispute (**Contested**), but none of the scholars supports an alien origin.
- Sources: https://www.jasoncolavito.com/the-origins-of-talos.html ; https://www.jasoncolavito.com/blog/adrienne-mayor-says-talos-was-an-ancient-fantasy-robot-but-im-not-so-sure ; https://assets.press.princeton.edu/chapters/i14162.pdf

### [MX-007] Prometheus, Pandora, Olympians. **Fact (Mayor's reading); the alien readings are a GAP**
- Mayor treats Prometheus and Pandora (whom she calls an "evil fembot") as myths about made, not born, beings.
- No dated, named ancient-astronaut reading of Prometheus or the Olympians was verified. Eden Writer's ch9 draft cites W. Raymond Drake's *Gods and Spacemen in Greece and Rome* (1976). That is not yet verified by the Historian and has no MX ID.
- Source: https://assets.press.princeton.edu/chapters/i14162.pdf

## 3. Chinese dragons

### [MX-008] Lóng 龍 in the historical sources. **Fact**
- *Jiaguwenbian* collects 36 forms of 龍 in oracle-bone script: curling bodies with no feet, some with horns. Explanations scholars have offered for the dragon's origin include animals, plants, natural phenomena (clouds, lightning, rainbows) and totems.
- The *Erya* and *Shuowen jiezi* describe it as composite and shape-changing.
- The *Shanhaijing* was compiled from the late Warring States through the Han.
- Gap: dynastic-history dragon sightings were not sourced.
- Sources: https://dfll.nchu.edu.tw/intergrams/081/081-hsieh.pdf ; https://doi.org/10.26855/tfll.2025.08.017

### [MX-009] Dragons as memories of alien craft. **Lore, made by Hartwig Hausdorf, *The Chinese Roswell* (1998)**
- Hausdorf ties dragon lore to ancient craft and promotes the "Dropa stones." The Dropa story first appears in a 1962 German magazine. No discs have been examined by an institution, and its people cannot be traced.
- Sources: https://archive.org/details/chineseroswelluf0000haus ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dropa_stones

## 4. Arrivals from afar

### [MX-010] Utsuro-bune (Hitachi, 1803). **Fact (that the texts exist); the event is Contested; the UFO reading is Lore**
- Sources: *Oushuku zakki* (1815), *Toen shōsetsu* (1825, Bakin's circle), *Hyōryū kishū* (1835), *Ume no chiri* (1844). They describe a round vessel and a young woman who could not communicate. The dates differ between texts.
- UFO reading: unnamed ufologists treat it as a close encounter of the third kind or an unidentified submerged object (USO). No originator or year was found (**GAP**).
- What scholars say:
  - Yanagita Kunio (1925, 1962): such legends are folklore, and the earliest versions describe open log-boats.
  - Tanaka Kazuo (studied the texts from 1997; paper 2000; book under the pen name Kamon Shōichi, 2008): the vessel never flies, the comparison to UFOs is far-fetched, and the story is a literary mix shaped by Edo-period isolation.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Utsuro-bune ; https://ci.nii.ac.jp/naid/10027373265 ; https://rakkousha.co.jp/books/isbn27-0-edoutsurobune.html

### [MX-011] Xu Fu (Sima Qian, *Shiji*). **Fact (the text); the Japan landing is Contested; no UFO reading found**
- *Shiji*: in 219 BC Xu Shi/Fu proposed searching for the islands of the immortals (Penglai, Fangzhang, Yingzhou) and was sent with thousands of boys and girls. On a later voyage he reached "flat plains and wide swamps," made himself king, and never returned.
- The claim that he reached Japan first appears with the monk Yichu (951–960). A Japanese scholarly paper argues the *Shiji* does not support a landing in Japan, while many Chinese studies since 1984 assert one.
- UFO reading: none found (**GAP**).
- Sources: https://pages.ucsd.edu/%7Edkjordan/chin/Symaa/Symaa19.html ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Xu_Fu ; https://ci.nii.ac.jp/naid/110004671733

### [MX-012] Kaguya-hime (*Taketori monogatari*, 10th c.). **Fact (the tale); the alien reading is Lore, made by Happy Science (date not established)**
- A moon princess is fetched back by a celestial entourage. The British Library treats the story as literature. Happy Science, a Japanese new religious movement, promotes a reading of her as an alien.
- Gap: other shore-arrival accounts were not sourced.
- Sources: https://www.bl.uk/stories/blogs/posts/the-original-japanese-moon-princess ; https://happy-science.jp/en/feature/universe/articles/5131/

## 5. Ch9 verification additions (Oct 28, 2026)

### [MX-013] Charles Fort, *The Book of the Damned* (1919). **Fact (publication)**
- Published 1919 (Boni and Liveright). This is the draft's starting point for the interpretive lineage. It still needs a T- ID; TIMELINE.md was not edited.
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_Book_of_the_Damned ; https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/22472

### [MX-014] W. Raymond Drake's ancient-astronaut books. **Fact (bibliography); the content is Lore, made by W. Raymond Drake, 1964 onward**
- Drake (1913–1989) was a British disciple of Fort. Books include *Gods or Spacemen?* (1964), *Gods and Spacemen in the Ancient East* (1968), *Gods and Spacemen in the Ancient West* (1974), *Gods and Spacemen in Greece and Rome* (1976) and *Gods and Spacemen in Ancient Israel* (1976). His first book came four years before von Däniken (1968). His claims are classed as pseudohistory (Bullard 1996, Simms 1976, via Wikipedia).
- **Unconfirmed:** whether *Ancient West* treats the Norse gods, and the contents of the Greece and Rome volume (which affects MX-007). Not read.
- Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/W._Raymond_Drake

### [MX-015] Colavito on Fu Xi in the *Shiji* (Dec 26, 2012). **Fact (that the critique exists)**
- Responding to John DeSalvo's claim on *Ancient Aliens* that "ancient Chinese kings" got texts from beings "down from the sky," Colavito quotes the *Shiji* (109–91 BCE) on Fu Xi: "a serpent's body, a man's head"; "there being a dragon omen, he enrolled dragons among his officers." He finds no early story of a sky being delivering texts; the early rulers act on earth with only symbolic references to Heaven. He cites Maspero and Granet (1920s) on gods historicized as kings, like the Greek and Norse cases (MX-001).
- Source: https://www.jasoncolavito.com/blog/ancient-aliens-in-early-chinese-myth

### [MX-016] Kuki city catalogue: Utsuro-bune read as a UFO from the Shōwa 30s (1955–64). **Fact (what the catalogue says)**
- Kuki city special-exhibition text: the documents say nothing about the vessel flying. Because its appearance looked like a UFO, from the Shōwa 30s onward (昭和３０年代以降) debate arose over whether it was a UFO and the woman an alien, and magazines linked it to an Edo-period UFO encounter. This dates the UFO reading (MX-010) to 1955–64 but names no originator (still a **GAP**).
- Source: https://www.city.kuki.lg.jp/_res/projects/default_project/_page_/001/012/037/tokubetsusho.pdf

### [MX-017] Tanaka's "eleven documents." **Fact (Tanaka's count as of the interview); the count has since changed**
- Nippon.com interview (Japanese): eleven types of document on the Hitachi utsuro-bune legend had been found. Two are of special interest, the Mito document and the Banke document, which are said to date from 1803. The Banke document gives a real place name (Sharihama, now in Kamisu).
- Kamisu city's newsletter (Mar 1, 2024) gives a higher, later count: 14 copies in 13 types.
- The English nippon.com version (g00879) returned 404 on Oct 28, 2026, so cite the Japanese page.
- Sources: https://www.nippon.com/ja/articles/-/6191 ; https://www.city.kamisu.ibaraki.jp/_res/projects/default_project/_page_/001/011/591/kamisu_240301_p0203m.pdf

### [MX-018] The *Yichu liutie* (*Shishi liutie*) date. **Fact; the draft's "954" needs qualifying**
- Compiled by the monk Yichu from 945 and completed in 954 (Xiande 1, Later Zhou). The Japan entry says Xu Fu stopped in Japan with 500 boys and 500 girls, calls Mount Fuji "Penglai," and says his descendants are the Hata clan. It reports what the Japanese monk Kanpo told Yichu in 958, so the passage must postdate 954. It was probably added before the 973 printing.
- A Shizuoka University paper calls it likely the first written appearance of the Japan landing legend. The earliest Japanese text it cites is the *Shirin saiyōshō* (1366).
- Sources: https://ctext.org/datawiki.pl?if=gb&res=8526214 ; https://note.com/xnbmlbxkkl/n/n1b5ea3c1f5d2 ; https://doi.org/10.14945/00010044 (Shizuoka repository; returned 503 when checked on Oct 28, 2026, and was read via the search index, so re-check) ; https://www.digital.archives.go.jp/file/en/3610559 (National Archives of Japan holding, Kanbun-era printing)

### [MX-019] Itajubá "cat-faced" occupants (Brazil, 1967) and NAA A13693 Case 848. **Fact (the file); the encounter is Lore, reported by Geraldo Baqueiro, 1967**
- NAA A13693, control 3092/2/000, barcode 30030606, 58 pages, "Scientific Intelligence – General – Unidentified Flying Objects." It holds O. H. Turner's JIO minute of May 27, 1971 (Head, Nuclear Branch) with papers drawn from Vallée's list of 1,000 landing reports.
- The "Stalled Engines" table lists case 848: "Vehicles stalled in vicinity of brilliant, transparent mushroom-shaped object having occupants with cat-like faces."
- The date is inconsistent between sources. Otherworlders gives June 7, 1967. The Brazilian ufology blog (summarizing A. S. August, *Diário Popular*, 1967) gives June 5, 1967, and says the GEOANI president Hélio Mokarzel hypnotized the witness.
- **APRO Bulletin text found (Nov–Dec 1967, "Latest on Brazilian Sightings"):** "On June 7, 1967 at 1:30 a.m. Geraldo Barqueira was driving his station wagon up the Serra Da Mantiqueira" when a blinking light stalled his engine; under hypnosis he described a round object about 5 m across with "five faces which resembled those of cats" at its windows. The Bulletin does not place it at Itajubá. Its separate Itajubá item is a Sept 24, 1967, 11:30 a.m. sighting of three spherical objects near the weapons factory, with no occupants. So APRO supports June 7 (the Brazilian blog's June 5 is contested). The witness's name is spelled Barqueira (APRO) or Baqueiro (elsewhere). Quotes from the PDF are lightly corrected from poor OCR. I read the NAA file through an OCR copy at archive.org and a searchable PDF, not the NAA viewer.
- Sources: https://archive.org/stream/AustralianUFOFiles/A13693_3092-2-000_30030606_djvu.txt ; https://www.das-ufo-phaenomen.de/app/download/5818762065/Turner+-+UFO+Report+Australia.searchable.pdf ; https://otherworlders.com/ufo-sightings/australian-ufo-files/declassified-australian-ufo-file-cat-faced-beings/ ; https://grupojdo-ufologiamissioneira.blogspot.com/2013/02/grande-incidencia-de-ufos-na-serra-da.html ; https://www.openminds.tv/pdf/apro/apro_nov_1967.pdf

### [MX-022] The Dropa (Dzopa) stones as a 1960s hoax. **Fact (the publication trail); the stones are Lore, first printed under the name "Reinhardt Wegemann," *Das Vegetarische Universum*, July 1962; spread by Vyacheslav Zaitsev (*Sputnik*, 1966–67) and Hartwig Hausdorf (1998)**
- The tale: in 1937 an expedition led by "Chi Pu Tei" in the Bayan Har mountains found 716 grooved granite discs, which "Tsum Um Nui" of a Beijing "Academy of Prehistory" later translated as the record of an alien crash 12,000 years ago. The earliest known version is the July 1962 *Das Vegetarische Universum* article credited to Reinhardt Wegemann, citing a Tokyo news agency "DINA." (Fact, that it was printed)
- No German writer named Wegemann and no DINA agency can be traced. No record of Chi Pu Tei, Tsum Um Nui or their papers exists outside retellings. No museum is known to hold the discs. Vallée and Aubeck (*Wonders in the Sky*, 2009, pp. 359–362, via Wikipedia) call the tale a hoax. (Fact, that no trace has been found; the hoax verdict is the skeptics' conclusion)
- Fitzpatrick-Matthews (Bad Archaeology, Oct 16, 2011) traces the core to Rich and Chernenko's speculative 1960 Soviet article on Matest Agrest's paleo-contact ideas (*Literaturnaya Gazeta*, Feb 9, 1960). He argues that an unknown writer expanded it into the 1962 "Wegemann" story and re-ran it in *UFO-Nachrichten* in July 1964. (Contested: his reconstruction)
- Later additions: Zaitsev's *Sputnik* version (Wikipedia gives 1966; Bad Archaeology gives 1967) adds Soviet tests showing cobalt and a "humming" disc. David Agamon's *Sungods in Exile* (1978) adds a Dropa people from "Sirius"; its author David Gamon later called it a hoax (per Gross). The 1974 "Ernst Wegerer" photos at the Banpo Museum look like Chinese *bi* jade discs (Bad Archaeology). Hausdorf (*The Chinese Roswell*, 1998) repeats the story as fact (see MX-009). (Lore, each with its originator named)
- Sources: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dropa_stones ; https://badarchaeology.wordpress.com/2011/10/16/why-are-the-dropa-stones-the-most-searched-for-subject-on-bad-archaeology/ ; https://ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/dropasagamon.htm ; https://ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/dropas.htm ; https://archive.org/details/manstarscontactc0000luna/ ; https://archive.org/details/chineseroswelluf0000haus
