# Modern UFO/UAP Cases: Fact Sheet for Eden Writer
*Compiled 2026-10-28 from live web search, plus the already-sourced TIMELINE.md in this folder. Documentary use.*

**Tags**
- **Reported fact:** the event or record is documented. This does not mean the object was extraterrestrial.
- **In between:** a real report whose nature is contested.
- **Lore:** an uncorroborated, hoaxed, or interpretive story.

**DISPUTED** marks contested points. **UNCONFIRMED** marks anything I could not verify this session. Witness backgrounds are given only as the sources state them.

---

## 1. [MC-001] Kenneth Arnold: June 24, 1947, near Mount Rainier, Washington
- **Tag:** In between. *The sighting and its coverage are documented; the objects were never identified.*
- **Witness background:** Private pilot with about 4,000 flight hours and a member of an Idaho search-and-rescue unit. He was flying a CallAir A-2 and searching for a crashed Marine C-46 when he saw nine objects (Smithsonian).
- **Aftermath:** Reporters Nolan Skiff and Bill Bequette of the *East Oregonian* wrote the first stories, and the AP wire made it national. The press coined "flying saucer," a phrase Arnold denied using in those words. He wrote a confidential report for the Army Air Forces (July 1947). He later said he had suffered "embarrassment... by misquotes."
- **Sources:** https://airandspace.si.edu/stories/editorial/1947-year-flying-saucer ; https://apnews.com/general-news-824242dbbda24a79bb224bd26abe39c8 ; https://www.ufologie.patrickgross.org/htm/arnoldrepaf.htm (text of the AAF report)

## 2. [MC-002] Roswell, including Maj. Jesse Marcel: early July 1947, near Roswell, New Mexico
- **Tag:** In between. *The debris recovery and the "flying disc" press release are fact. The alien-craft narrative is DISPUTED.*
- **Witness background:** Maj. Jesse Marcel, intelligence officer of the 509th Bomb Group at Roswell Army Air Field. Irving Newton was the weather officer who identified the wreckage as a balloon and RAWIN target (1994 USAF report).
- **Aftermath:** The AAF retracted the disc story and called it a weather balloon. In February 1978 the now-retired Marcel told Stanton Friedman the debris was extraterrestrial and the balloon story a cover (**DISPUTED**). The 1994 USAF report traced the debris to a Project Mogul balloon and quotes Newton as saying Marcel tried to convince him that markings on the debris were "alien writings." AARO (2024) found no evidence of recovered extraterrestrial material. A museum and tourism economy grew up from 1991.
- **Sources:** https://www.nsa.gov/portals/75/documents/news-features/declassified-documents/ufo/report_af_roswell.pdf ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jesse_Marcel ; https://www.aaro.mil/Portals/136/PDFs/AARO_Historical_Record_Report_Vol_1_2024.pdf
- **Unconfirmed:** No verbatim transcript of the 1978 Friedman interview was found.

## 3. [MC-003] Capt. Thomas Mantell: January 7, 1948, Godman Field (Fort Knox), crash near Franklin, Kentucky
- **Tag:** Reported fact. *A documented fatal military crash during a UFO intercept. The object is probably identified but not proven.*
- **Witness background:** Age 25, Kentucky Air National Guard, 165th Fighter Squadron, flying an F-51D. A WWII veteran with 2,867 flight hours, the Distinguished Flying Cross, and four Air Medals (DVIDS). Godman commander Col. Guy Hix and tower personnel also saw the object.
- **Aftermath:** He climbed above safe altitude without oxygen, lost consciousness, and crashed. The Air Force first suggested Venus, then balloons. Blue Book head Edward Ruppelt later concluded the object was most likely a classified Navy Skyhook balloon, though no launch record for that day was found (**DISPUTED** by some researchers). It made front-page news, and DVIDS (2023) says "questions remain."
- **Sources:** https://www.dvidshub.net/news/436733/questions-remain-75-years-after-mysterious-fort-knox-ufo-incident-downed-pilot ; https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Page:Ruppelt_-_The_Report_on_Unidentified_Flying_Objects.djvu/66 ; https://www.nicap.org/docs/mantell/analysis_mantell_randle.pdf

## 4. [MC-004] Washington, D.C. sightings: July 19–20 and 26–27, 1952, Washington National Airport and the Capitol area
- **Tag:** In between. *Radar and visual reports are documented; the temperature-inversion explanation is contested.*
- **Witnesses:** Air traffic controllers, pilots, and radar operators (mostly unnamed in the sources found).
- **Aftermath:** Jets were scrambled, and headlines read "Saucers Swarm Over Capital." On July 29, 1952, Maj. Gen. John A. Samford held a Pentagon press conference citing inversions and acknowledging that about 20% of reports were unexplained. The CIA convened the Robertson Panel in January 1953.
- **Sources:** https://www.history.com/articles/ufos-washington-white-house-air-force-coverup ; https://www.nicap.org/waves/pressconf_1952.htm ; https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/cia-role-study-UFOs.pdf

## 5. [MC-005] Betty and Barney Hill: September 19–20, 1961, U.S. Route 3, White Mountains, New Hampshire
- **Tag:** Lore. *The report and the "missing time" are documented, but the abduction details emerged under hypnosis with no physical corroboration.*
- **Witness background:** An interracial couple from Portsmouth, NH, and civil rights activists (BPL, Wikipedia). They were hypnotized separately by psychiatrist Dr. Benjamin Simon.
- **Aftermath:** The Boston Traveler published the story without their consent (Oct 25, 1965). John G. Fuller's *The Interrupted Journey* followed in 1966, excerpted in *Look*, and NBC's TV film *The UFO Incident* aired in 1975. Amateur astronomer Marjorie Fish linked Betty's "star map" to Zeta Reticuli (**DISPUTED**). A state historical marker was placed in 2011. The couple's papers are at UNH.
- **Sources:** https://www.bpl.org/blogs/post/the-abduction-of-betty-and-barney-hill/ ; https://archives.unh.edu/repositories/3/resources/107 ; https://www.hmdb.org/m.asp?m=74571

## 6. [MC-006] Lonnie Zamora at Socorro: April 24, 1964, about 1 mile SSW of Socorro, New Mexico
- **Tag:** In between. *A police officer's report with physical ground traces; Blue Book left it "unidentified."*
- **Witness background:** Socorro city police officer. Blue Book director Maj. Hector Quintanilla called him "a serious police officer, a pillar of his church."
- **Aftermath:** Dr. J. Allen Hynek visited on April 29 and said "hoax is ruled out." He noted that no radar had tracked the object. Quintanilla called it "the best-documented case on record." The site became a local tourist attraction.
- **Sources:** https://www.cia.gov/resources/csi/static/Investigation-of-UFOs.pdf ; https://socorro-history.org/HISTORY/PH_History/200808_socorro_ufo.pdf ; http://iapsop.com/archive/materials/nicap_reporter/nicap_reporter_v2_n08_may_1964_i20.pdf

## 7. [MC-007] Malmstrom AFB and Robert Salas: March 16, 1967 (Echo Flight); Salas's account is "on or about" March 24, 1967 (Oscar Flight), Montana
- **Tag:** In between. *The Echo Flight missile shutdown is documented fact. The link to a UFO is DISPUTED.*
- **Witness background:** Robert Salas was a 26-year-old Air Force lieutenant and missile launch officer in 1967 (Great Falls Tribune via Wikipedia). Col. Walter Figel (Echo) reportedly recalled a guard describing a "large round object" (per Robert Hastings, a pro-UFO researcher). Capt. Eric Carlson (Echo) recalled no UFO reports.
- **Disputed points:**
  - (a) Salas originally tied his story to Echo, then relocated it to Oscar Flight around March 24. There is no comparable public outage file for Oscar.
  - (b) The Echo unit history states that "rumors of UFOs... were disproven."
  - (c) Skeptics attribute the Echo outage to a power or transformer fault, but another archival summary says commercial power switching was ruled out and EMP or electrostatic effects were considered possible.
  - (d) Claims that a 2025 Pentagon/AARO report attributed the outage to a classified EMP test are **UNCONFIRMED**. One source says the 2025 report does not establish this.
- **Aftermath:** Salas went public in the Great Falls Tribune on Aug 13, 1996. He took part in a 2010 National Press Club event organized by Robert Hastings (per Hastings).
- **Sources:** https://skeptoid.com/episodes/842 ; https://uaprad.org/witnesses/robert-salas/ ; https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Malmstrom_UFO_incident ; https://www.theufochronicles.com/2010/09/echooscar-witch-hunt.html
- [MC-017] **Later appearances:** BBC Suffolk (Dec 2010) quotes Salas as saying he discussed the case with Col. Halt at a 2010 conference: http://news.bbc.co.uk/local/suffolk/hi/people_and_places/history/newsid_9301000/9301923.stm . AARO's 2024 report says it interviewed five former USAF ICBM personnel, including Malmstrom, but doesn't name them. **Unconfirmed:** Salas's books.

## 8. [MC-008] Travis Walton: November 5, 1975, near Turkey Springs, Apache-Sitgreaves National Forest, Arizona
- **Tag:** Lore. *Testimony and polygraphs only; no physical evidence.*
- **Witness background:** Walton was 22, a logger on a Forest Service thinning contract. Crew boss Mike Rogers, age 28. Crew: Ken Peterson, John Goulette, Steve Pierce, Allan Dalis, Dwayne Smith.
- **Aftermath:** Walton was missing until just after midnight on Nov 11, when he called from a phone booth in Heber. The state polygraph on Nov 10, designed mainly to rule out foul play, judged five crew members truthful and one inconclusive. Walton's own results conflict: skeptic Philip J. Klass reported a Nov 15 "gross deception" result, while a Feb 1976 test was reported as passed. Walton published *The Walton Experience* (1978), which became the film *Fire in the Sky* (1993, with fictionalized abduction scenes). A 50th-anniversary event was held in Heber in 2025.
- **Sources:** https://www.wmicentral.com/news/fire-in-the-sky-50-years-later-the-abduction-tale-that-still-divides-the-white/article_f48b2c7f-65bf-4a2c-bd96-45e467234f25.html ; https://realufo.org/case/travis-walton ; https://rr0.org/time/1/9/7/6/06/WaltonAbductionCoverUpRevealed_UfoInvestigator/

## 9. [MC-009] Rendlesham Forest: December 26–28, 1980, near RAF Woodbridge/Bentwaters, Suffolk, UK
- **Tag:** In between. *An official memo exists, but the lighthouse is a strong mundane explanation.*
- **Witness background:** Lt. Col. Charles Halt, USAF deputy base commander, wrote the memo "Unexplained Lights" (Jan 13, 1981) and recorded a tape. USAF security policemen John Burroughs and Jim Penniston say they saw a craft on the first night (BBC 2010).
- **Aftermath:** The memo is in UK National Archives file DEFE 24/1948. BBC reporting matches the five-second flashes on Halt's tape to the Orford Ness lighthouse, and skeptics read the "landing marks" as rabbit diggings. The memo's dates are off from the reported events.
- **Sources:** https://discovery.nationalarchives.gov.uk/details/r/C10342055 ; http://news.bbc.co.uk/local/suffolk/hi/people_and_places/history/newsid_9301000/9301923.stm ; http://ianridpath.com/ufo/appendix.html

## 10. [MC-010] JAL Flight 1628: November 17, 1986 (Alaska Standard Time), over eastern Alaska, from the Canadian border to south of Fairbanks
- **Tag:** In between. *A documented FAA investigation; it could neither confirm nor refute the sighting.*
- **Witness background:** Capt. Kenji (also spelled Kenju or Kenjyu) Terauchi was flying a JAL 747 cargo flight from Iceland to Japan via Anchorage. UPI reported it was the third UFO sighting of his 29-year career. Three crew in total.
- **Aftermath:** The FAA released its file on March 5, 1987. It found the second radar target was a split return from the 747 itself, and a diverted United jet saw nothing else. FAA spokesman Paul Steucke said, "We are accepting the description of the crew but are unable to support what they saw." Terauchi reported another sighting on Jan 11, 1987.
- **Sources:** https://nara-media.s3.amazonaws.com/seattle/rg-237/733667/box_1/733667-001-024/733667-001-024.pdf (FAA release at NARA) ; https://www.upi.com/Archives/1987/03/06/Final-FAA-report-cant-explain-UFO-sighting/4541542005200/ ; https://documents.theblackvault.com/documents/ufos/jal1628/733667-001-023.pdf
- [MC-018] **Grounding:** Within months of the publicity, JAL reportedly moved Terauchi to a desk job; he was reinstated several years later (Anchorage Press; KB42 citing Bruce Maccabee). No primary JAL record has been found. Sources: https://www.anchoragepress.com/news/unfriendly-skies-the-extraordinary-flight-of-jal-1628-alaska-s-best-known-ufo-encounter/article/_8e2d3270-f9d5-11e9-b9db-7ba9229138ae.html ; https://kb42.info/index.php/Japan_Air_Lines_Institutional_Response

## 11. [MC-011] Phoenix Lights: March 13, 1997, Arizona
- **Tag:** In between. *Flares and an A-10 formation explain much of it; partly DISPUTED.*
- **Witness background:** Thousands of residents. Gov. Fife Symington.
- **Aftermath:** In July 1997 the Air National Guard attributed the lights to flares (Maryland ANG A-10s, Operation Snowbird). In 1997 Symington staged a press-conference joke with an aide in an alien costume. In 2007 he said he had seen a delta-shaped craft himself.
- **Sources:** https://www.nytimes.com/1997/07/27/us/flares-caused-old-sky-lights-air-guard-says.html ; https://www.cnn.com/2007/TECH/science/11/09/simington.ufocommentary/index.html ; https://www.deseret.com/1997/6/20/19318941/alien-encounter-leaves-media-agog/

## 12. [MC-012] Bob Lazar: November 1989 broadcasts (and Dec 9, 1989), KLAS-TV, Las Vegas; the claimed site is "S-4" near Groom Lake, Nevada
- **Tag:** Lore. *Credentials unverified and claims uncorroborated.*
- **Witness background, as claimed (DISPUTED):** Physicist, with master's degrees from MIT and Caltech. **Verified:** a 1982 Los Alamos lab phone directory listing and a 1982 Los Alamos Monitor profile. MIT and Caltech have no record of him, and EG&G had no record.
- **Aftermath:** The broadcasts made Area 51 famous. Jeremy Corbell's documentary followed in 2018 and the Joe Rogan interview on June 20, 2019, then the viral "Storm Area 51" event (Sept 2019). AARO (2024) called claims of reverse-engineering programs inaccurate.
- **Sources:** https://weightofevidence.org/cases/lazar-claims-1989 ; https://www.papooselake.org/interview-transcripts/1989-on-the-record-with-george-knapp ; https://www.theguardian.com/us-news/2019/sep/24/area-51-raid-event-what-happened-nevada

## 13. [MC-013] USS Nimitz "Tic Tac" (Fravor, Dietrich, Day): November 14, 2004, about 100 mi SW of San Diego
- **Tag:** Reported fact. *Multiple named military witnesses, radar, and DoD-released video; the object remains unidentified.*
- **Witness backgrounds:**
  - Cmdr. David Fravor: TOPGUN graduate and commander of the F/A-18F squadron on the Nimitz.
  - Lt. Cmdr. Alex Dietrich: F/A-18F pilot, who first spoke publicly in 2021.
  - Senior Chief Operations Specialist Kevin Day: USS Princeton air-intercept control supervisor. In his own interview he says he spent his career as an Aegis SPY radar operator and that this was his last deployment.
- **Aftermath:** The pilots say there was no formal investigation at the time. The FLIR video leaked in 2007, was published with the NYT story in 2017, and was officially released by DoD on Apr 27, 2020. Fravor and Dietrich appeared on *60 Minutes* (May 2021). Fravor testified on July 26, 2023. Day gave his first on-camera interview to History's *Unidentified*.
- **Sources:** https://www.cbsnews.com/news/navy-ufo-sighting-60-minutes-2021-05-16/ ; https://www.history.com/articles/uss-nimitz-2004-tic-tac-ufo-encounter ; https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=jC_IuxZ6qT4 ; https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/2165713/statement-by-the-department-of-defense-on-the-release-of-historical-navy-videos/

## 16. [MC-016] Luis Elizondo: resigned October 2017; NYT story Dec 16, 2017; IG complaint May 2021; testified Nov 13, 2024
- **Tag:** In between. *His Pentagon service and public role are documented. Whether he led AATIP is DISPUTED.*
- **Background:** Career counterintelligence specialist, assigned in 2008 to work for the Pentagon UAP program (POLITICO). Former OUSD(I) staff member. The NYT described him as having run the program.
- **The dispute:**
  - The Pentagon, via spokespeople Christopher Sherwood and Susan Gough (2019), said "Mr. Elizondo had no assigned responsibilities for AATIP while he was in OUSD(I)." Gough added that DIA administered AATIP, that Elizondo "interacted" with it, and that "he did not lead it."
  - Elizondo's June 3, 2020 email to Gough, released under FOIA, says "I ran the program" and lists supporters including Harry Reid and Christopher Mellon.
  - In May 2021 he filed a 64-page DoD IG complaint alleging disinformation and reprisal. [MC-019] The DoD IG closed the reprisal case on Feb 16, 2022, finding no action affecting his clearance (letter released via FOIA to The Black Vault: https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/dod-inspector-general-releases-transcript-of-luis-elizondo-interview-much-more/).
- [MC-020] **Aftermath:** He joined To The Stars Academy. He testified at the House hearing on Nov 13, 2024, introduced there as an author; his memoir *Imminent: Inside the Pentagon's Hunt for UFOs* came out in August 2024 (https://www.nytimes.com/2024/08/16/books/booksupdate/imminent-luiz-elizondo.html).
- **Sources:** https://www.nytimes.com/2017/12/16/us/politics/pentagon-program-ufo-harry-reid.html ; https://www.theblackvault.com/documentarchive/pentagon-reinforces-mr-luis-elizondo-had-no-responsibilities-on-aatip-senator-harry-reids-2009-memo-changes-nothing/ ; https://www.esd.whs.mil/Portals/54/Documents/FOID/Reading%20Room/Public_Affairs/21-F-0938-PA-Elizondo-Emails.pdf ; https://www.politico.com/news/2021/05/26/ufo-whistleblower-ig-complaint-pentagon-491098 ; https://www.congress.gov/event/118th-congress/house-event/117721

## 30. [MC-030] PURSUE Release 07: Oct 23, 2026, Department of War (war.gov/UFO); headline "matte black triangle" seen near a secure U.S. facility (2026, month and day redacted)
- **Tag:** Reported fact (the release and the reports exist) / In between (what was seen).
- **Details:** Chief Pentagon spokesman Sean Parnell announced the seventh PURSUE release (Presidential Unsealing and Reporting System for UAP Encounters), made under the President's 2026 order. Per Axios, the batch had 10 documents. An FBI "Unidentified Aerial Object Observation Report" and a DoD UAP report describe three federal agents who saw a silent "matte black triangle" with a wingspan of about 15 ft. It rose vertically up to about 300 ft above a ridgeline, hovered about 20 s, flew about 1.5 mi in about 3 s, and vanished. A thermal monocular detected no "discrete heat sources associated with propulsion". The records draw no conclusion about origin or threat. The same tranche includes a declassified 2010 Skinwalker Ranch report and 1949–1963 items.
- **Sources:** https://www.war.gov/News/Releases/Release/Article/4632058/department-of-war-publishes-seventh-release-of-unidentified-anomalous-phenomena/ ; https://www.axios.com/2026/10/23/new-ufo-documents-flying-triangle ; https://www.war.gov/ufo/
